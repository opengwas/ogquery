**The indices a query answers with are scoped to an index space, and the entry points a cached integer can reach take the space it came from and refuse a mismatch (opengwas/ogquery#13, opengwasdb ADR 0109 §2).** A splice renumbers `analysis_index` and `variant_index`, so an index cached from another release is still in range and names a different Analysis or variant without failing. An `IndexSpace` is the Store and release (`ReleaseIdentity`) and the axis it numbers (`variant`, a Hybrid's `variant:panel` rows, `analysis`), so two Stores that share a `release_id`, and a Hybrid's panel and shared numberings, are told apart. **Constructors keep their signatures:** `VariantAxis`, `PanelVariantAxis`, `DenseRhoReader`, `TopHitTier(s)`, `RaggedCSRReader`, `ByVariantReader`, `CsrReader`, `DenseGridReader` and `AnalysesIndex` learn their identity from the manifest of the release they open (a Hybrid's Dense Component is scoped to its Hybrid), and refuse with `ReleaseIdUnknown` where none describes their store; an optional `identity=` is accepted only where no manifest is reachable, and refused where one says otherwise. Ids that are missing, empty or the text `None` refuse (`StoreManifest` is the one parser; a null `release_id` no longer becomes `'None'`). **What breaks is the keyword**, required and keyword-only, on: `VariantAxis` and `PanelVariantAxis` `by_index`, `by_indices`, `identity_by_indices`, `rsids_by_indices`, `source_alids_by_indices`, `rows_by_indices` (and `VariantAxis.sidecar_records`), `expect_space=`; `DenseRhoReader.row`, `pair`, `matrix`, `IndexedSubset.decode_analysis`, `ByVariantReader.rows_for_variant`, `RaggedCSRReader.span`, `expect_space=`; `TopHitTier.read` and `clumps` (`expect_space=`, required when an `analysis_index` is given); `RaggedCSRReader.segment_positions`, `eaf_pairs`, `n_pairs`, `pair_positions`, and `read`/`inspect` of `DenseGridReader`, `CsrReader` and `SubsetReader`, `expect_spaces=`. A mismatch raises `ogquery.guard.ReleaseMismatchError` (a `ValueError`) naming both. `Associations` carries `variant_space` and `analysis_space` outside its mapping view, so no digest changes; `resolve` and `resolve_rows` refuse a result stamped with another space or none, and `Associations.concat` refuses records of different spaces. Also: `digests.Reference` serialises (`to_json`, `from_json`).
