**ogquery reads format 0.4.0** (opengwas/ogquery#19; opengwasdb #517, #525; spec §2, §4.0, §5, §19, §21; ADR 0108 §8-§9). A release of the new 0.4 series, keyed by CPRA and signed for ALT, opens and answers; a package without the series refuses it by name.

- **The series.** `0.4` is in `SUPPORTED_FORMAT_VERSIONS`, `ZARR_FORMAT_BY_SERIES` and, as `0.4.0`, `WRITTEN_FORMAT_VERSIONS`; `format_version_for(cpra_keys=True, signed_for_alt=True)` stamps it and refuses either without the other. `carries_cpra(version)` is the one split between the CPRA names and the ALID ones: a release is read by its series, never by which files exist.
- **The join guard.** `ogquery.format.CpraNormalForm`, `cpra_normal_form(manifest)`, `require_joinable(a, b)`, `CpraNormalFormMismatch` and `MixedCpraNormalForms`. A 0.4.0 manifest must hold exactly 0.4.0's `cpra_normal_form`, on GRCh38, and declare `provenance.reference_sequence` and `provenance.admission` in §2's types (anything else is refused at open, `UnsupportedCpraNormalForm`); a Hybrid's two manifests must declare them equal; an earlier release derives its form from its stamp and may not declare one, not even as `null`. `StoreRelease.cpra_normal_form` and every `Associations` answer expose it. `open_release` records each release's form and warns `MixedCpraNormalForms` once per pair of forms.
- **The names.** The `variant_cpra_*` and `variant_source_cpra_*` sidecars (`ogquery.cpra_sidecar`), a 0.4.0 table row decoded by its series, and a record, `variants_table()` row and resolved row of `chromosome`, `position`, `ref`, `alt`, `cpra`, `rsid`, `source_cpra`, in that order, with no `effect_allele`. 0.2 and 0.3 keep their names and columns.
- **The query contract.** A variant is asked for by rsid (`rs` and digits, recognised positively) or CPRA; any other identifier raises `MalformedIdentity`. `phewas` and `lookup` both report misses. A CPRA `trim_alleles` would change raises `NotNormalCpra`; a malformed one raises as a malformed ALID does. An absent CPRA gives no row, and an SNV whose swapped spelling names a row, or an absent indel, gives a `VariantMiss`: on `Associations.misses` and, from `variants()`, as `VariantMissWarning`.
- **Not yet.** An rsid query, or a row's rsid, on a 0.4.0 release raises `RsidIndexNotYetSupported`: its rsid and alias indexes are #524's, and no layout is decided.
