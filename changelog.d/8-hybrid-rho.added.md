A Hybrid release's Rho reads come from its Dense Component's Rho Matrix (opengwasdb#495, #496; opengwasdb PR #500): `rho()`, `rho_row()` and `rho_matrix()` on a Hybrid refuse an Overflow-only Analysis by name (`OverflowOnlyAnalysisError`), never answer a NaN for it, and a Rho Matrix that does not record its Overflow-only Analyses, or records them wrongly, is refused. The release digests carry a `rho` section (`ogquery.digests`). The contract bundle is refreshed to opengwasdb `355449d7` (bundle 2): it adds a Rho Matrix to the Dense and Hybrid releases and two releases, `dense_rho` and `hybrid_overflow_only`, so ogquery's Rho readers are now under the contract. The change is ported from opengwasdb under ADR 0001's rule for a change made before opengwasdb#475; the public-name list is regenerated from `355449d7`. This gives 0.4.0.
